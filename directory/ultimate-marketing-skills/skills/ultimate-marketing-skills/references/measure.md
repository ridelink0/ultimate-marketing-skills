# Measure: the weekly loop

Marketing that is not measured is a guess repeated. Once a week:

1. **Collect.** For every item posted seven or more days ago, record views,
   average watch time or completion, likes, comments, shares or sends, saves,
   profile visits, and the one number that matters for the project (wishlists,
   installs, signups, orders). Read them in each platform's analytics, or
   from connected MCP servers (Supermetrics, Google Analytics, Amplitude,
   HubSpot) when installed.
2. **Compare with the account's own median**, not with other accounts.
   Feeds are heavy-tailed: most posts do little and a few do most of it.
3. **Write** `.ums/reports/<date>.md`:
   - The top three posts and the one thing they share (hook, format, topic).
   - The bottom three and what to stop.
   - Next week: two repeats of what worked, one new test.
4. **Feed it back.** The next calendar starts from that report.

## What to count

| Project | The number | Leading signals |
|---|---|---|
| Game | Wishlists per week | Shorts views, profile clicks, Steam page visits |
| App | Installs that open twice | Store page views, conversion rate, video sends |
| SaaS / devtool | Activated signups | Site visits from social, demo views, replies |
| Local | Calls, directions, visits | Profile views, review count |
| Shop | Orders, repeat orders | Saves, link clicks |

Never report views as success on their own. Never compare a week to a
viral outlier.

## Paid boosts

Only boost a post already in the account's top tenth by watch time or saves,
with a daily cap the person approved. Judge it by cost per wishlist, install,
signup or order.
