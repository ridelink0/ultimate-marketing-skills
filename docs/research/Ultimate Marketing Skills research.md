# Ultimate Marketing Skills must draft fast, verify everything, fake nothing

A Claude Code marketing plugin in November 2026 should be a verified-knowledge and production layer with human gates, not an autopilot that posts. Every major platform now ranks content by predicted viewer behavior (watch, finish, send, reply) and publishes few weights, so the plugin should teach the proxies and tell the user which numbers are official and which are lore. The social-first era also hardened originality rules: Instagram extended its anti-aggregator penalty to photos and carousels on **30 April 2026** ([Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)), LinkedIn said on **12 March 2026** it demotes comment automation and engagement pods ([LinkedIn News](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/ImprovingTheFeed)), and Hacker News still bans automated posting and AI-edited text ([HN Guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html)). The video stack is code-first: HyperFrames (Apache-2.0) or Remotion for composition, faster-whisper for captions, ffmpeg to finish, and generative clips only as short inserts. Most case-study numbers are self-reported, and no audited app or SaaS case was found. Existing skills cover frameworks well: coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT, 53 skills) is the best base, and the plugin should add dated platform specs, a video pipeline and compliance gates. The law sets hard limits: the FTC fake-review rule, EU AI Act Article 50 (in force since 2 August 2026) and UK ASA/CMA rules. The directory path is concrete: submit one plugin folder at claude.ai/directory/manage, with a README of 40+ words, a LICENSE, pinned launchers and no binaries. One policy line needs a check before submission, because Anthropic's Software Directory Policy reportedly excludes AI image, video and audio generation.

## Short-form feeds reward predicted satisfaction, and originality now gates reach

TikTok says its For You feed sorts videos by a prediction score built from the likelihood of like, share, comment, skip, finish and favorite, with a final similarity check that can swap out consecutive near-duplicates ([TikTok transparency](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system/)). The page lists signal groups (your behavior, similar users, context, video attributes including posting time and soundtrack) but no weights, and it excludes search, so the plugin cannot say how TikTok search ranks anything. Instagram's Adam Mosseri names watch time, likes and sends as the top Reels signals, with sends slightly more useful for reaching non-followers, and advises judging by rates per viewer rather than raw totals ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)). The "sends are worth 3-5x likes" figure traces to a third party and should not be encoded.

The sharpest change is originality. Instagram's April 2026 expansion excludes accounts that primarily post unoriginal photos or carousels from recommendations, though their followers still see them, and minimal edits such as watermarks or speed changes do not count as original ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)). Press coverage adds that eligibility returns after 30 days of mostly original content, which is secondary ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)). A "10 unoriginal posts in 30 days" threshold circulates but appears in neither the TechCrunch article nor Instagram's page. The practical rule for the plugin is to cross-post from a clean, watermark-free master, rewrite captions per platform, and favor founder footage, demos and devlogs that the user shot.

YouTube's Shorts rules are the best documented. Shorts run up to three minutes if square or vertical; any Short over one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked worldwide, which makes trending TikTok audio a real risk on longer Shorts ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)). Since 31 March 2025 a view counts whenever a Short starts or replays, and monetization is based on "engaged views" ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070)). YouTube publishes no ranking weights; vidIQ's claim that 13-second and 60-second Shorts perform best is secondary and carries no method.

Length and format data conflict by metric, so the plugin should pick by goal. Socialinsider's 23.6 million brand videos (January to June 2026) put TikTok engagement highest under 30 seconds (6.00%) and Reels engagement highest at 45 to 60 seconds, but the denominators differ, so cross-platform rates are not comparable ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/short-form-video-statistics/)). Buffer's analysis of 4 million Instagram posts found Reels earned 36% more reach than carousels while carousels earned 12% more engagement ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)). On TikTok engagement stays roughly flat (5.25% at 1,000 to 5,000 followers, 4.50% at 100,000 to 1 million) while views climb, so small brands are not structurally disadvantaged ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/short-form-video-statistics/)). Hook advice ("state the payoff in three seconds") rests on vendor data and one second-hand TikTok figure; no platform-primary retention benchmark exists, so the plugin should present hook patterns as hypotheses to test with TikTok Studio, YouTube's viewed-versus-swiped-away view and Instagram's Trial Reels.

Specs the plugin can encode with a "verified on" date are narrower than blogs suggest. The safe baseline is 9:16 at 1080x1920, H.264, 30 fps. Meta's ad guide asks creators to keep the top 14%, bottom 35% and each side 6% of a Reels ad free of key elements ([Meta Ads Guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels)), Pinterest publishes pixel margins ([Pinterest](https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs)), TikTok offers safe-zone templates only as downloads, and no official organic safe zone exists for any of the three platforms. TikTok's API takes captions up to 2,200 characters and uploads up to 4 GB and 10 minutes ([TikTok API](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post), [media guide](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-media-transfer-guide)); other consumer-app limits (TikTok app uploads, Instagram Reels length, Bluesky video) could not be read from primary pages and must be flagged "unverified". Threads allows 500 characters, 5 links per post and a 10,000-character text attachment ([Threads API](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/threads/posts)). X counts 280 weighted characters with every URL as 23 ([X docs](https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/counting-characters)). Bluesky's 300-grapheme limit and LinkedIn's 3,000-character post limit were not confirmed on official pages.

## Text and community platforms punish promotion and reward specific, human replies

X's ranking is the only one with open code: the final score is a sum of weights times predicted action probabilities, with negative weights for blocks, mutes and reports, then an author-repeat decay, an out-of-network discount and a new-author boost ([xai-org/x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm)). The implication is that replies, dwell and shares outweigh likes and that mass-posting has diminishing returns per post. The old "a reply is worth 150 likes" figure comes from the 2023 release, not this code ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/decoding-xs-algorithm-heres-how-grok-open-sources-and-curates-the-for-you-feed-511751-2026-01-20)). Link deboosting is disputed: X's head of product called the claim fake in April 2026 and advised placing links in the post body ([Roboin](https://roboin.io/article/en/2026/04/13/x-denies-link-deboost-recommends-in-post-links/)), while an outside analysis reports 94% fewer views on link posts ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/x-drops-year-old-link-penalty-musk-tells-zuckerberg-on-platform/)). The plugin should recommend an A/B test on the user's own account and assert neither claim. X reach is heavy-tailed: Buffer's February 2025 data show a median of 4 interactions per post but a mean of 328 ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/x-threads-bluesky-data/)), so one breakout is not a repeatable result.

LinkedIn's own words are the reliable layer. Its March 2026 post says LLMs now help the feed judge what a post is about, and that it targets "comment automation, engagement pods, and unauthorized third-party tools" and "comment to agree" bait ([LinkedIn News](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/ImprovingTheFeed)); its policies bar agreeing in advance to like or re-share each other's content ([LinkedIn Policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies)). The widely repeated numbers (document carousels at 6.6% engagement, links cutting reach 50 to 60%, a "360Brew" 150-billion-parameter model) have no named primary source and are excluded. The defensible advice is founder-led personal posting with real insight and no automation.

Reddit's spam policy says promotion alone is not spam but defines spam as repeated or unsolicited actions, automated or manual, and notes that some communities enforce a 10% self-promotion rule ([Reddit Help](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)). Subreddit rules are the enforced layer, so the plugin should make rule-reading and disclosure a precondition and never draft cross-posted link drops. Reddit is also an AI-citation surface (about 130.3 million daily users in Q2 2026 per [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/reddits-user-base-grows-to-more-than-130-million-daily-actives/826783/)), which explains the brand rush, though citation-share studies disagree widely and one saw Reddit's ChatGPT share swing from about 60% to 10% within weeks ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138)).

Discord changed on 16 November 2026: each Discoverable Server (1,000+ members, 8+ weeks old) gets a public web page, and from mid-December public-channel messages may appear without usernames, on by default except in the EU, UK, Canada, Brazil, Australia and South Korea ([Discord blog](https://discord.com/blog/making-content-from-discoverable-servers-easier-to-discover)). Playtest and NDA channels belong outside Web Presence. Discord's developer guidance for game servers is concrete (one role at onboarding, test builds as scheduled events, one developer reply a day) but names no studio and gives no numbers ([Discord Developer Docs](https://docs.discord.com/developers/game-development/how-to-create-a-community-for-your-game)).

Hacker News and Product Hunt are launch spikes with strict rules. Show HN requires something runnable that the poster can discuss, bars sign-up pages and landing pages, and forbids soliciting votes ([Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html)). A study of 138 AI repo launches found 121 GitHub stars in 24 hours on average and no statistical advantage from the "Show HN" tag after controls, with timing mattering ([arXiv 2511.04453](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.04453v1)). Product Hunt staff say daily rank is "most points", points being upvotes plus meaningful engagement, with gaming tracked and removed ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/producthunt/how-product-of-the-day-week-month-are-chosen)); the account-age and first-hours claims circulating are unverified. Threads and Bluesky show low medians (Feb 2025: Threads 5, X 4, Bluesky 3 interactions per post) ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/x-threads-bluesky-data/)), and the fit of Bluesky for developers is opinion.

Search itself moved. Pew found a 15% click rate on standard results without a Google AI summary and 8% with one ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)); Ahrefs puts the position-one CTR drop at 58% relative, Seer at 61%, and SparkToro at 68% of searches ending without a click ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/), [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update), [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/); the latter three are search-summary reads). On llms.txt, Ahrefs found 97% of about 38,000 valid files received zero requests in May 2026 ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/97-of-llms-txt-files-got-no-requests-ahrefs-data-shows/)), so the plugin should treat answer-engine work as SEO plus brand mentions and promise no citations.

## The video pipeline is code-first, and generative clips are inserts

Composition should run on HyperFrames or Remotion. HyperFrames is Apache-2.0 with "no per-render fees or commercial-use thresholds", needs Node 22+ and FFmpeg, and the repo was created 10 March 2026 ([GitHub](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes)). Remotion is free for individuals and companies of three or fewer people; above that, Creators costs $25 per seat per month, Automators $0.01 per render with a $100 monthly minimum, and Enterprise starts at $500 per month ([Remotion pricing](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing.md)). Revideo is MIT ([GitHub](https://github.com/redotvideo/revideo)). Playwright can record scripted demos, but file size must be set explicitly and output is compressed ([Playwright](https://playwright.dev/docs/api/class-screencast)). The plugin should default to HyperFrames for zero licence friction and document Remotion as the alternative with its eligibility terms.

The rest of the chain is cheap and local. faster-whisper (MIT) returns word timestamps and is up to four times faster than openai-whisper ([faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper)); Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) is the zero-cost voice ([Kokoro](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro)); ElevenLabs charges $0.04 to $0.08 per 1,000 characters and licenses its music for commercial use on Starter and above ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api)). The suggested order is script and hook, voiceover, word-timed captions, visuals, composition at 1080x1920 and 30 fps, render, two-pass loudnorm to a -14 LUFS default, per-platform exports, QA and disclosure toggles. This sequence is the notes' synthesis; no source documents it end to end and the ffmpeg recipes were not smoke-tested, so the plugin must test them before shipping.

Generative video is volatile. The Sora 2 and Sora 2 Pro APIs shut down on 24 September 2026 with no one-to-one replacement ([OpenAI deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations)), and Runway retired the Gen-3 Alpha Turbo and Gen-4 Aleph APIs on 30 July 2026 per an aggregator ([aiagent-navi](https://aiagent-navi.com/special/video-gen-api-pricing/)). Clips run only 4 to 20 seconds, so they work as b-roll inserts stitched in the composer. Prices belong in a config file with a "last verified" date, since the Veo and Kling figures are secondary. For a 24 GB GPU, Wan 2.2's 5B model is the open-weight option ([Wan2.2](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2)). Music has a hard rule: TikTok business accounts are limited to the Commercial Music Library, the licence covers TikTok only, and trending sounds are not cleared for brands, though the evidence is vendor blogs and a Social Media Today report ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-changes-rules-on-music-usage-by-businesses/577734/)). The plugin should bake licensed audio into the render and log the licence per asset.

Store and trailer specs are fully primary-sourced. Apple app previews run 15 to 30 seconds, up to 500 MB, with three previews per device size ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-preview-specifications)); Apple screenshots take 1 to 10 images per device ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/screenshot-specifications)). Google Play wants a 512x512 icon, a 1024x500 feature graphic, 2 to 8 phone screenshots and a YouTube preview URL ([Play Console](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151)). Steam capsules are Header 920x430, Small 462x174, Main 1232x706 and Vertical 748x896, with screenshots at least 1920x1080 and old capsule sizes dropped since August 2024 ([Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets)). Apple's page states an iPhone Duo screenshot requirement from April 2027, which needs a recheck.

Visual and ad research has fewer clean doors than expected. Meta's `ads_archive` API returns political and issue ads plus ads delivered in the EU, not global commercial ads, and needs an access token ([Meta reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/ads_archive/), [tools page](https://transparency.meta.com/en-us/researchtools/ad-library-tools)). TikTok's Commercial Content Library covers the EEA, Switzerland and UK without an account ([TikTok support](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/personalized-ads-and-data/commercial-content-library)), while its API needs reviewed access. Scraping terms are contested, and X, Snapchat and Pinterest repositories are weak per independent audits ([Mozilla](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/blog/investigation-every-tech-giants-ad-oversight-tools-fall-short-in-historic-election-year/)). The plugin should drive public UIs for single lookups, treat Foreplay (about $59 to $459 per month), Atria and MagicBrief as user-supplied logins, and record platform, advertiser, URL, capture date and status for every reference. It should extract patterns, not reuse assets, and flag fair use as a question for counsel because no source addressed it.

Measured visual evidence is thin but usable as hypotheses. Meta and Toluna report that ads establishing brand and message in the first five seconds were 1.7 times as likely to drive strong purchase intent ([Toluna](https://tolunacorporate.com/what-makes-short-form-video-ads-work-meta-toluna-study/)); TikTok and Vidmob work links strong hooks to twice the engagement ([Vidmob](https://www.vidmob.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Vidmob-x-Tik-Tok-Hook-Analysis-.pdf)). Thumbnail data are outlier-only: vidIQ found 69% of 500 outlier thumbnails showed a face, with no baseline ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/)). YouTube's Test & Compare picks winners by watch-time share, supports up to three titles or thumbnails, and excludes Shorts ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)); that is the right instrument to recommend over any thumbnail rule.

## Named cases show vertical clips plus a festival or creator beat, and most numbers are self-reported

Case evidence is uneven by project type, and the verified notes narrow what the plugin may cite. For indie games, DREADMOOR's CMO reported about 200,000 wishlists, with +44,000 in a month around an IGN Fan Fest trailer, +13,000 in a week from vertical content plus a preview, and creator posts giving 25,000+ in a week; the listed spikes do not clearly sum to the total, and the figures are self-reported ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/05/21/case-study-how-dreadmoor-reached-200000-wishlists-and-where-they-came-from)). Tiny Terraces posted 132 Shorts with 1.1 million views yet its biggest one-day spike (1,500 wishlists) came from the Next Fest demo feature, and the dev reported about 28,000 pre-launch wishlists and $26,799 gross in the first month ([Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/how-much-can-you-earn-as-an-indie-dev-tiny-terraces-creator-share/zb889b)). The Matriarch's developer says one TikTok moved wishlists from about 400 to 20,000+ and questions how well TikTok wishlists convert ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/from-400-to-20-000-wishlists-with-1-video-how-tiktok-can-make-your-game-go-viral)); its date was not retrieved. Tiny Glade's one-million-wishlist claim rests on a Substack summary. No Reddit-led indie case with numbers was found.

Next Fest data give the plugin its planning logic. A survey of 208 developers from the February 2025 fest, dated 26 March 2025, found fest lift starts at roughly 2,000 existing wishlists and that median gains rise with the starting base ([HowToMarketAGame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/03/26/archive-january-2026-benchmarks-how-many-wishlists-can-i-get-from-steam-next-fest/)). That page also lists non-player shares of 68% to 88%, but the verification pass found no text source for the range, so the plugin should say only that most fest wishlists come from shoppers who never play the demo; a later post puts the demo-player-to-wishlist median at 19.3% ([howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/06/30/nobody-plays-demos-and-that-is-ok/)). Alinea's October 2025 cohorts convert 1.3% to 5.5% of wishlists to buyers within weeks, a four-game sample with estimated sales ([Alinea](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/wishlist-to-buyer-conversions-for)). Co-op breakouts Content Warning and Peak sold through streamers rather than any attributed TikTok campaign.

For apps, the best-documented case is Cal AI: about 250 creators on fixed monthly fees, brand-owned accounts, and an acquisition by MyFitnessPal in December 2025, with revenue claims ranging from $30 million to over $50 million ARR across third-party sources ([Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/)). The 2025 faceless-slideshow and founder cases are interview claims: NaturalWrite reports about $100,000 in 90 days with copied viral formats reaching 1.5 million views against 150,000 for originals, and its monthly revenue fell from $41,000 to $27,000 ([Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/our-simple-app-made-100k-in-90-days)); Dan Kwon's cases (Kunch AI, Arise, Shepherd) are self-reported and relayed by a vendor blog ([Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/he-made-usd2-2m-from-building-simple-apps-heres-how-no-code)). No app with audited store data was found, and the copy-and-iterate tactic collides with Instagram's originality rule. Apple custom product pages show only vendor-measured uplifts (8.6% to 23%) ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/do-apple-ads-custom-product-pages-work/)).

For SaaS and developer tools the evidence is thinnest. The arXiv study above is the only quantitative launch data; PostHog's founder blog describes seeding GitHub stars before an HN launch ([PostHog](https://posthog.com/blog/after-the-hn-launch)); Indie Hackers accounts call a launch "an event" rather than an acquisition strategy. Build-in-public examples such as Pieter Levels took roughly a decade to build an audience ([SoftwareSeni](https://www.softwareseni.com/building-in-public-the-10-year-distribution-strategy-behind-solo-founder-revenue/)). No verified revenue case was retrieved, so the plugin should frame launches as spikes and push repeatable channels (comparison pages, free tools, programmatic SEO). For local businesses, Judy's Family Cafe went to 10.5 million views and owner-quoted sales up 50% ([Marketing Brew](https://marketingbrew.com/stories/2024/10/08/small-businesses-are-getting-creative-on-social-media-and-driving-foot-traffic-as-a-result)), and The Nitro Bar cites 60% growth without a baseline ([Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/how-the-nitro-bar-became-a-tiktok-sensation)); Google Business Profile evidence is agency or Google promotional material, and Whitespark's survey ranks the primary category and proximity first for the local pack ([Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors)). Positioning frameworks (April Dunford's five components, Reforge's growth loops) are confirmed but secondary to execution ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/a-guide-to-advanced-b2b-positioning), [Reforge](https://www.reforge.com/blog/growth-loops)).

## Fold in marketingskills and Anthropic's plugin, wrap MCP servers in scanners and approvals

Anthropic's marketing plugin (knowledge-work-plugins) is Apache-2.0 with seven commands, five skills and ten connectors, but it is generic: social posts are "Hook, Body, CTA, 3-5 hashtags" with no per-platform limits, and its skill content last changed in March 2026 ([GitHub](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/tree/main/marketing), [content-creation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/main/marketing/skills/content-creation/SKILL.md)). It is licence-clean to adapt with attribution. The deeper base is coreyhaines31/marketingskills: MIT, confirmed at 53 skills ([GitHub](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills)), whose `social` skill tags algorithm claims verified versus reported and bans AI-tell prose ([x-algorithm.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/main/skills/social/references/x-algorithm.md)). Strip its partner promotions when adapting. ECC (MIT, 328 skills) supplies `crosspost`, whose rule against identical copy and untrusted-source section are worth copying, plus a marketing agent ([ECC](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC)). The recommended move is to fold in structure from these three, not to re-vendor all 53 skills, and to add what none has: dated per-platform spec tables, a measurement loop tied to real data, the end-to-end video pipeline with an approval gate, an AI-tell lint, and compliance gates for FTC, ASA, email and music rights.

| Do not vendor | Why |
|---|---|
| Postiz (postiz-app, postiz-agent) | AGPL-3.0; call it as an external tool only ([postiz-agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gitroomhq/postiz-agent/main/LICENSE)) |
| Remotion code | Custom licence, free only up to three people ([LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/remotion-dev/remotion/main/LICENSE.md)) |
| remotion-dev/skills | No licence file found ([GitHub](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills)) |
| X xmcp | No licence file; last push 9 April 2026 ([xmcp](https://github.com/xdevplatform/xmcp)) |

On MCP servers, the platform picture is lopsided. Meta's and TikTok's official servers are ads-side with no documented organic publish tool; X's self-run xmcp can post but is unlicensed and its docs and repo disagree; no official LinkedIn, YouTube or Instagram posting server was found (per [Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/research/social-media-mcp-servers), a vendor with a competing product). Vendor-hosted servers exist for Buffer (`https://mcp.buffer.com/mcp`, write-capable, auth type disputed between its pages) ([Buffer](https://developers.buffer.com/guides/integrations/mcp)), HubSpot, Canva, Klaviyo, Similarweb and Ahrefs, plus Apache-2.0 local servers for Google Analytics and Google Ads ([GA](https://github.com/googleanalytics/google-analytics-mcp), [Ads](https://github.com/googleads/google-ads-mcp)). Reddit and Bluesky community servers are stale (last pushes in 2025). The plugin should not ship its own posting server; it should detect what is connected and fall back to "draft plus manual publish".

Claude Code now does much of the work that gateways once did. Tool definitions are deferred by default through tool search, tool output is capped at 25,000 tokens, and plugin servers use the name `mcp__plugin_<plugin>_<server>__<tool>`, which hook matchers must use ([Claude Code MCP docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp)). OAuth cannot complete under `claude -p`, so scheduled jobs need header or key auth. Anthropic reports a 98.7% token cut (150,000 to 2,000) by filtering data in code rather than the context window ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/code-execution-with-mcp)), which matters most for GA4, ads and Ahrefs exports; it also recommends few, namespaced tools with concise response modes ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents)). Among add-on tools, Cisco's mcp-scanner (Apache-2.0, YARA mode needs no key), Snyk's agent-scan (Apache-2.0, needs a token and sends metadata to Snyk) and the official MCP Inspector for developing the plugin's own servers are worth recommending ([Cisco](https://github.com/cisco-ai-defense/mcp-scanner), [Snyk](https://github.com/snyk/agent-scan), [Inspector](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/inspector)); Docker's MCP Gateway (MIT) adds container isolation ([Docker](https://docs.docker.com/ai/mcp-gateway/)). MetaMCP is archived and should not be recommended. Composio and Pipedream hold user tokens on a hosted service, which is a lock-in and trust cost, and Zapier was not researched.

The security design that follows is that the combination of reading untrusted social text, reading private CRM or ads data, and holding a publish or spend tool should never share a session without per-write approval. The plugin should split a read-only research agent from a gated publisher, pin `oauth.scopes` to read where the vendor allows, and treat fetched posts as data in every skill prompt ([Claude Code MCP docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp)).

## Automate drafts, schedules and analytics; keep voice, spend and disclosure human

Official write APIs exist almost everywhere, with caps that differ from folklore. X is pay-per-use ($0.015 per post, $0.200 with a URL, a 13-fold difference a budget guard must price in) ([X docs](https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing)). YouTube allows 100 uploads per day by default ([YouTube quota](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/guides/quota_and_compliance_audits)). Instagram allows 100 API-published posts per rolling 24 hours, and the plugin should read the live endpoint and stay well below it ([Instagram](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)). Threads allows 250 ([Threads](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/posts/)). LinkedIn's Posts API needs `w_organization_social` for page posts and the approval path is not documented ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/posts-api)). TikTok is the hard case: unaudited clients post privately only, and even audited Direct Post requires a preview, explicit user approval, no preselected privacy values, and no promotional watermarks or links ([TikTok guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines)). Fully autonomous TikTok publishing is therefore non-compliant by design.

Platform rules draw the line between safe and restricted automation. Scheduled, original posts to the user's own accounts through official APIs are the safe class. X bans keyword-triggered replies and automated likes (secondary snippets, since the help page returned 403), and LinkedIn prohibits any third-party software that automates activity on its site ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387)). HN prohibits automated posting and AI-edited text, and Reddit ties spam to repetitive and unsolicited behavior. Browser automation is not a workaround: Anthropic says users remain responsible for Claude's browser actions including third-party automation terms, and posting is not on its list of confirm-required actions, so the plugin needs its own approval step before any submit click ([Anthropic Help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12902428-using-claude-in-chrome-safely)). The recommended autonomy matrix is a plugin policy, not platform text: autonomous for scheduled original posts and read-only analytics; approval for every TikTok post, URL posts, replies, DMs, comments, member-profile posts, anything paid, any AI-realistic media; never for fake reviews, bought engagement, keyword-triggered engagement, vote actions, or duplicate multi-account posting. A daily cap of 1 to 3 posts per platform, an immutable audit log and a kill switch are design choices with no published threshold behind them.

The legal floor is firm. The FTC's 16 CFR Part 465 took effect 21 October 2024 and bans fake and AI-generated reviews, review buying and fake social indicators such as bot followers ([Federal Register](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-08-22/html/2024-18519.htm)); the civil penalty is **$53,088** per violation ([Federal Register 2025](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-01-17/html/2025-01361.htm)). The plugin must refuse to generate fabricated testimonials. The FTC Endorsement Guides were not fetched, so "#ad" duties should be cited only after verification. EU AI Act Article 50 applies since 2 August 2026: the corrected scope means a new generative system placed on the market after that date owes machine-readable marking now, while only older systems get until 2 December 2026, and fines reach EUR 15 million or 3% of turnover (law-firm commentary; the Official Journal text was not read) ([Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/viewpoints/2026/08/102nfqm/you-talkin-to-me-operationalising-the-eu-ai-acts-transparency-obligations), [Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/en/spain/intellectual-property/art/digital-omnibus-ai-has-been-published)). The safe default for EU-facing output is to mark generated media now. In the UK, the ASA and CAP require a prominent "Ad" or "#ad" on each ad post, and the DMCC Act's fake-review ban has applied since 6 April 2025 ([ASA](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/influencers-guide.html), [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/10/23/new-influencer-guidance-from-the-asa-cap-and-cma-what-has-changed-and-what-to-d-102o6ar)). CAN-SPAM carries up to $53,088 per email ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)); GDPR and PECR consent rules were not researched. Platform labels add to the law, not replace it: YouTube asks for an altered-or-synthetic flag on realistic content ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)), and TikTok and Meta auto-label C2PA-marked content, though whether ffmpeg re-encoding strips C2PA is unverified.

## Directory submission is a validator checklist, and two policy lines need a pre-check

Submission runs through claude.ai/directory/manage ("Submit new", then "Plugin bundle") from a GitHub repo, requires a Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise plan, and treats each plugin folder as its own submission ([Submit](https://claude.com/docs/plugins/submit.md), [Publish](https://claude.com/docs/directory/publish.md)). Skills cannot be submitted alone and must sit in a bundle. The GitHub account must be connected to the Claude organization and able to push to the repo; the repo may be private until listing but must be public before it goes live. The limit is 10 submissions per organization per 24 hours, and pull requests to anthropics/claude-plugins-official are not monitored ([README](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-plugins-official)). Each version gets validation and a security scan, and a person reviews a new listing before it goes live; statuses run Draft, Scanning, Needs changes, In review, Approved, Published, and "Approved" does not mean installable ([Status](https://claude.com/docs/directory/submission-status.md)).

The portal's Validate button checks far more than `claude plugin validate`, which covers only well-formedness ([Checklist](https://claude.com/docs/plugins/pre-submission-checklist.md)). The thresholds that matter for this plugin:

| Rule | Exact requirement | Outcome if broken |
|---|---|---|
| Manifest | `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` present; set `name`, `description`, `author`, `version` | Block if missing manifest; warning for missing fields |
| Name | lowercase letters, digits, hyphens, up to 64 characters; not a reserved word; distinctive; no claim to be official | Block if taken or reserved; hold if look-alike |
| README | at least 40 words outside code blocks | Block |
| License | LICENSE file or `license` in plugin.json | Block |
| Size | repo under 50 MiB archived, under 256 MiB unpacked, under 10,000 files; each plugin file under 5 MiB | Validation stops |
| Reviewer hold | over 512 files, any non-image file over 256 KiB, binaries (`.ico`, `.pdf`, `.zip`, executables) | Held for a human |
| Launchers | `npx`, `uvx` and similar must pin exact versions (`pkg@1.2.3`, `pkg==1.2.3`) | Block |
| Credentials | none in files; use `userConfig` with `sensitive: true` and `${user_config.KEY}` | Block |
| Remote MCP | `https://` or `wss://` URL, type `http`, `sse` or `ws` | Block |
| Hook and MCP commands | full path from `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}`; no inline programs such as `python3 -c` | Block |
| Layout | exact spelling `hooks/`, `skills/`, `SKILL.md`; no symlinks, submodules or LFS | Block |

The plugin's name "ultimate-marketing-skills" fits these rules and avoids the reserved prefixes `claude-`, `anthropic-` and `cc-plugin-` ([Manifest reference](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins-reference)). The directory reads `icon`, `documentationUrl`, `supportUrl`, `privacyPolicyUrl` and `termsOfServiceUrl` only from plugin.json; icon size rules and whether `privacyPolicyUrl` is a hard block were not found, so treat the privacy policy as required for any plugin that touches a service. The portal also asks for data-handling answers (personal data, third-party sends, retention, under-18 use) and four acknowledgements. A first-try pass is skills as Markdown, optional remote https `.mcp.json`, no scripts, no binaries, no `bin/` folder, fewer than 512 files, and a README that lists everything the plugin runs, sends or fetches. A complete README does not make a behavior allowed, and source must be readable, not minified.

Two items sit outside the validator and need attention. First, the Anthropic Software Directory Policy, as summarized by a fetch tool and so needing a live check, bars hidden or encoded instructions, dynamic pulling of behavioral instructions from external sources, and requires a privacy policy link, a testing account and at least three working example prompts; it also lists exclusions including ads or sponsored content, AI image, video or audio generation (with a narrow exception), and autonomous money movement without a human in the loop ([Policy](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145358-anthropic-software-directory-policy)). A marketing plugin whose pipeline generates video and audio, and that touches ad spend, may collide with those lines, so the safest design routes media generation through the user's own tools and keeps spend out of scope. Second, a remote MCP server the bundle references must be submitted separately as an MCP connector. For a second distribution target, OpenAI's portal converts `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, but commands must become skills and hooks must be removed, so a dual-target plugin should ship skills only ([OpenAI guide](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/guides/submit-claude-plugin.md)). Typical review duration and the full security-scan category list were not found.

## Conclusion

The research changes what "marketing skills" should mean. The durable asset is a dated, source-labeled knowledge base that marks each claim as official, measured or lore, because the most quoted numbers (X reply multipliers, LinkedIn percentages, hook retention figures, the Next Fest non-player range) failed the second check or never had a primary source. A plugin that quietly encodes them would teach confident errors, while one that carries a "verified on" date and a source URL per row would be rare among the repos surveyed. The verification pass also shows why the plugin should read live quota and policy endpoints (Instagram's publishing limit, YouTube's upload bucket, TikTok's creator info) instead of storing numbers that platform pages themselves contradict.

The second insight is that platform rules and law converge on one design: original material, authored by a human, disclosed correctly. Instagram's originality rule, LinkedIn's engagement-pod demotion, HN's AI-text ban, TikTok's required review of each post and the FTC and EU labeling duties all penalize the same failure, which is scaled, unreviewed, synthetic output. The plugin's job is to compress research, drafting, rendering and scheduling so that the human's remaining work is judgment: approving the post, replying in the thread, and signing off the ad label. That design also fits the directory's own constraints, so the plugin can satisfy users, platforms and reviewers with the same architecture. Unresolved items to close before shipping are the YouTube unverified-project lock, the X ranking release date, Official Journal text for Art. 50, Veo official pricing, and the Software Directory Policy wording on generated media.
