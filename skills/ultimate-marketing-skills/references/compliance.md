# Compliance: disclosures, AI labels, reviews, music, email

This is a working guide, not legal advice. When money, health, finance,
children or another country's law is involved, the person checks with
someone qualified. Checked 2026-11-25.

## Never (these are why the forbidden list exists)

- **Fake or bought reviews and testimonials, AI-written reviews presented as
  customers', bought followers or views.** US: FTC rule 16 CFR Part 465,
  effective 21 October 2024; civil penalties up to $53,088 per violation
  (16 CFR 1.98, January 2025). UK: banned since 6 April 2025 under the DMCC Act.
- **Sockpuppets and vote or engagement manipulation.** Banned by every
  platform UMS targets (X, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn,
  Hacker News, Product Hunt).

## Disclose

- **Paid, gifted or affiliate posts**: a clear "Ad", "#ad" or the platform's
  paid-partnership label in the post itself, visible before "more". UK ASA/CAP:
  a bio statement is not enough. The UMS queue flags `--ad` items without one.
- **AI-generated or altered realistic content**: TikTok's AI-generated label,
  YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting, Meta's AI info. EU AI Act
  Article 50 transparency duties apply from 2 August 2026; the machine-readable
  marking duty in 50(2) is postponed to 2 December 2026 only for generative
  systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026. UMS flags `--ai` items.
- **Claims**: "#1", "guaranteed", "clinically proven", savings figures and
  comparisons need proof you can show. The copy audit flags the usual ones.

## Music and footage

- Business accounts: licensed music only. TikTok's Commercial Music Library
  covers TikTok business use; YouTube's Audio Library covers YouTube.
- Other people's clips, streams and screenshots: ask, credit, and keep the
  permission in `.ums/research/permissions.md`.

## Email

- US CAN-SPAM: honest sender and subject, a postal address, a working
  unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days; up to $53,088 per violating
  email. EU and UK: consent before marketing email.

## Claude's own boundaries

Claude does not enter passwords, payment details or one-time codes, does not
create accounts, and does not move money. Spend items in the queue are
approved and paid by the person.
