# Search and AI answers

Search still sends buyers, but fewer clicks per search. Pew Research
(22 July 2025): people clicked a result 8% of the time when a Google AI
summary appeared, against 15% without. Vendor studies (Ahrefs, Seer,
SparkToro) agree on the direction and disagree on the size; treat their
percentages as directional.

## What to write

- Pages people and answer engines quote: "X vs Y", "alternatives to Y",
  how-to with real steps and screenshots, pricing in plain text, a FAQ that
  answers what support actually gets asked.
- One page per question. A clear first sentence that answers it.
- Structured data where it is true (Product, SoftwareApplication, VideoGame,
  FAQ only for real FAQs).

## Where answer engines read

Reddit threads, YouTube videos, review sites, documentation and comparison
pages. Being mentioned well in those places matters as much as your own site.
Vendor citation studies disagree and swing month to month; do not optimise
for one engine's current favourite.

## What not to bother with

- llms.txt for Google: Google's AI features guidance says no new
  machine-readable or AI text files are needed. Large studies found no
  citation benefit elsewhere either.
- Keyword stuffing, doorway pages, mass AI-written pages: they are spam
  under Google's policies and get a site demoted.
