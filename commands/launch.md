Use the `ultimate-marketing-skills` skill for everything below and follow its rule zero:
nothing is published, sent or paid for unless `scripts/policy.mjs` allows it for the
workspace's mode. No emoji anywhere.

The text typed with this command is the request (Claude Code appends it below as
ARGUMENTS; in Codex it is the rest of the message).

`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}` is this plugin's folder. Codex leaves it empty: put the folder
that holds `.codex-plugin/` in its place in every command.

1. Check the store or landing page against references/platforms.md (Steam capsules, App Store
   previews, share card).
2. Draft, in `.ums/launch/`: Show HN post and first comment, Product Hunt listing and maker
   comment, posts for the one or two subreddits whose rules allow it (quote the rule),
   X and Bluesky posts, LinkedIn founder post, Discord announcement, creator emails.
3. Make the launch video (references/video.md).
4. Write `.ums/launch/run-sheet.md`: hour by hour, who posts what where.
5. Queue the X and Bluesky items. Everything else is posted by a person: Hacker News, Product
   Hunt, Reddit and LinkedIn ban automated posting.

Never ask anyone for upvotes and never use more than one account.
