# Privacy

Ultimate Email Skills runs on your machine. Gev operates no server,
analytics or telemetry for it. Drafts, built HTML and text stay in the folder
you choose. When you sign in to Klaviyo, Resend or MailerLite through /mcp and
approve a send, the email goes to that service under its own terms. Claude
processes the conversation under your Claude plan's terms.
